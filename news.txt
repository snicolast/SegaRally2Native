# Sega Rally 2 Native: what is new, for the players.
#
# The game reads this file from GitHub (main branch) at launch. When it has an
# entry with a higher number than the news.txt in the player's folder, the main
# menu shows those entries in an UPDATE AVAILABLE window with UPDATE NOW
# (update.bat) and LATER. Commits that do not add an entry show nothing, so
# several fixes can go out together: add the entry when they are all in.
#
# Format:
#   [N] title      starts entry N (a number one higher than the last; the
#                  title is usually the date)
#   - text         a line of the entry (lines starting "- " get a bullet)
#   # ...          a comment, never shown
# Newest entry at the top. Keep each one short: about ten lines fit.

[13] 13 November 2026
- New installs on NVIDIA cards start with DLAA (DLSS QUALITY on screens above 1440p) instead of MSAA 4X, which was too heavy for 4K at 120 Hz. Saved settings are not changed.
- NicoLauncher now shows your achievements on the game's page: start the game once from the launcher and exit, and the row appears.
- Started from a terminal, the game gives the prompt back when it ends.
- Stutters: out/slow.log now says where the time went in each one. Attach it if you report one.

[12] 13 November 2026
- Intel graphics (UHD, Iris): the game should now start instead of stopping with "Could not start the 3D renderer".

[11] 13 November 2026
- Steering wheels: Fanatec and other DirectInput wheels should now be detected, with force feedback, and the wheel is listed under SETTINGS > CONTROLS.

[10] 13 November 2026
- New DIFFICULTY option in the new SETTINGS > GAME tab: from VERY EASY to VERY HARD, the arcade's own setting for the time you get at the start and at each checkpoint.

[9] 12 November 2026
- New HD HUD option in SETTINGS > GRAPHICS: turn it off to race with the arcade's original pixel HUD and screens instead of the redrawn ones.

[8] 12 November 2026
- NicoLauncher is out: one free app that installs, builds, updates and starts this game and the other recomps. No Visual Studio needed
- Get it at github.com/snicolast/NicoLauncher-Hub. Your current install keeps working, and the launcher can use the folder you already have

[7] 12 November 2026
- Menu music now plays through the game's own sound output: no more silent menus on some PCs.
- Smoother picture: no more brief jump back when a frame arrives late, as when a stage starts.
- Practice mode: the frames round the course pictures on the course select screen are gold again instead of plain white.

[6] 12 November 2026
- Attract demos: the cars now sit on the road instead of hovering over it, and keep their windows in place.
- Smooth motion in the attract demos and replays: the wheels no longer show through the bodywork of a car coming fast at the camera.

[5] 12 November 2026
- Closer to the arcade: the cars' white paint no longer looks grey, with or without ray tracing, and sunny stages are no longer darkened.
- BUY ME A COFFEE has moved to MORE RECOMPS.

[4] 11 November 2026
- Shadows: without ray tracing the cars have the arcade's shadow underneath again, so they no longer seem to float.
- Without ray tracing, the sun's shadows have smooth edges instead of blocky steps.

[3] 11 November 2026
- No more console window behind the game.
- Building: fixed a Visual Studio setup error on PCs with an unusual PATH, and the NVIDIA DLSS download is now optional.

[2] 11 November 2026
- Engine sound fixed: the engines play through the sound chip's effects processor, which a bug in the emulation kept silent. Thanks to the Reddit player who noticed!

[1] 11 November 2026
- 14 achievements, from your first finish to champion of the championship.
- RECORDS is back in the main menu, with ACHIEVEMENTS and STATS tabs: races, championships, best laps and times on each course.
- MORE RECOMPS now lists Star Wars Trilogy too.
